# KINGAIBOT Product Definition / 产品定义

## English

KINGAIBOT is a future-facing autonomous-agent execution system developed by kingai.work as a long-term project. It is deliberately separated from the existing KING AI intelligent-lifeform main system during its early development lifecycle.

Its strategic role is to become the **terminal execution layer of KING AI**: the controlled runtime that receives authorized missions and carries them out through tools, services, devices, workflows and cooperating agents.

KINGAIBOT focuses on execution reliability, policy enforcement, durable state, tool interoperability, security isolation, recovery, observability and controlled learning. The KING AI main system remains responsible for the broader intelligent-lifeform experience, high-level memory, governance, identity and strategic intelligence.

The two systems are therefore complementary rather than competing.

Official website: https://kingai.work  
Contact: vip@kingai.work

## 中文

KINGAIBOT 是 kingai.work 面向未来长期研发的自主智能体执行系统。在早期研发阶段，它与现有 KING AI 智慧生命体主系统保持独立，以便独立验证安全、稳定性和执行能力。

它的战略定位是成为 **KING AI 的终端执行层**：接收经过授权的 Mission，通过工具、服务、设备、工作流和其他智能体完成真实数字世界中的执行。

KINGAIBOT 重点解决执行可靠性、权限策略、持久状态、工具互操作、安全隔离、自我恢复、可观测性和受控学习。KING AI 主系统继续承担更高层的智慧生命体体验、记忆、治理、身份和战略智能。

因此两个系统不是竞争关系，而是未来的上下层协同关系。
